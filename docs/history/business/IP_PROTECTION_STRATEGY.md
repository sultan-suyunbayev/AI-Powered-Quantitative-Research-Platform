# Intellectual Property Protection Strategy — CustodiaCloud (High-Level)

**Document Version:** 2.1  
**Date:** 2025-12-18  
**Classification:** Internal Strategy Document

**Canon (committee-safe language / what we must not claim):** `docs/DOCUMENTATION_CANON_DESIGN.md`.

**Non-legal notice:** This document is informational and must be reviewed with qualified counsel before external-facing use.

---

## 1) Principles

- Protect core differentiation primarily through **engineering execution** and **trade secrets**.
- Treat patents as an **optional, counsel-led** strategy; do not use “patent pending” wording unless verified and counsel-approved.
- Do not imply that IP mechanisms confer regulatory compliance.

---

## 2) What we protect (examples)

- CCEA implementation details (Cloud/Agent boundary enforcement patterns)
- Research/simulation tooling and operational workflows
- Governance/evidence export patterns and internal process know-how

---

## 3) Mechanisms

### 3.1 Trade secrets (default)

- Access control, least privilege, security hygiene
- Internal documentation and controlled distribution of sensitive implementation details

### 3.2 Contractual protections

- Confidentiality (NDAs where appropriate)
- Contractor/employee IP assignment and confidentiality provisions (counsel-reviewed)

### 3.3 Patents (optional)

- Evaluate novelty and business value with counsel
- If pursued, keep all timelines and budgets **TBD** until verified

---

## 4) What we avoid stating (external narrative)

- Dollar valuations of “IP value”
- “No prior art”, “USPTO search completed”, or “patent pending” claims without counsel-reviewed evidence
- Any statements implying CustodiaCloud provides investment advice, portfolio management, or execution-as-a-service
