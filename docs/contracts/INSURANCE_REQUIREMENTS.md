# Insurance & Indemnification Requirements
## DORA Contractual Protection

**Version**: 1.0
**Date**: 2025-01-17
**Status**: Template / Target Requirements (Illustrative)
**Legal Reference**: DORA Article 28, Industry Best Practice

> **Note**: This document describes target coverage requirements and illustrative provisions. Actual coverage must be verified via certificates of insurance. This is not a claim of current coverage—actual policies are maintained separately and provided to contracted clients upon request.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines target insurance coverage requirements and indemnification provisions designed to support DORA contractual requirements and protect both Provider and Client interests in ICT service arrangements.

## 4. Client Requirements

### 4.1 Standard Requirements

For all clients, Provider maintains:
- Minimum €5M cyber liability coverage
- Minimum €5M professional liability coverage
- A-rated or higher insurers
- Annual certificate of insurance on request

### 4.2 Enterprise Requirements

For Enterprise clients or those with higher exposure:
- Increased coverage limits negotiable
- Named insured endorsement available
- Primary and non-contributory endorsement available
- Waiver of subrogation available

### 4.3 Regulatory Client Requirements

For DORA-regulated financial entity clients:
- Coverage specifically includes regulatory defense costs
- Coverage for third-party provider failures included
- Notification of material coverage changes (30 days)
- Certificate of insurance provided annually

## 5. Certificate of Insurance

### 5.1 Standard Certificate Contents

Certificates of Insurance provided to clients include:
- Policy numbers and effective dates
- Coverage types and limits
- Insurer names and ratings
- Named insured information
- Cancellation notification provision (30 days)

### 5.2 Request Process

1. Client requests certificate via client portal or email
2. Provider issues certificate within 5 business days
3. Annual renewal certificates sent automatically
4. Material coverage changes notified within 30 days

### 5.3 Sample Certificate Language

```
CERTIFICATE OF LIABILITY INSURANCE

This certificate is issued as a matter of information only and confers
no rights upon the certificate holder. This certificate does not amend,
extend or alter the coverage afforded by the policies below.

INSURED: [Provider Legal Name]
         [Provider Address]

COVERAGES:
- Cyber Liability: €10,000,000 (Primary + Excess)
- Professional Liability: €5,000,000
- General Commercial Liability: €2,000,000

CERTIFICATE HOLDER: [Client Name]
                    [Client Address]

Should any of the above described policies be cancelled before the
expiration date thereof, notice will be delivered in accordance
with the policy provisions.
```

## 6. Indemnification Provisions

### 6.1 Provider Indemnification

Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Client from:

| Claim Type | Scope | Cap |
|------------|-------|-----|
| Third-party IP infringement | Provider's services/platform | Per contract |
| Data breach caused by Provider | Direct damages | Per contract |
| Provider's gross negligence | All damages | Per contract |
| Provider's willful misconduct | All damages | Unlimited |
| Personal injury/property damage | Provider's personnel | Insurance limits |

### 6.2 Client Indemnification

Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider from:

| Claim Type | Scope |
|------------|-------|
| Client's use of services | Violations of law/regulation |
| Client's data | Third-party claims regarding Client data |
| Client's instructions | Compliance with specific Client instructions |
| Client's negligence | Claims arising from Client's negligence |

### 6.3 Mutual Indemnification

Both parties shall indemnify each other for:
- Breach of confidentiality obligations
- Violation of applicable law
- Misrepresentation in the agreement

### 6.4 Indemnification Process

1. Notice: Prompt written notice of claim
2. Control: Indemnifying party controls defense
3. Cooperation: Indemnified party cooperates
4. Settlement: No settlement without consent
5. Expenses: Indemnifying party bears costs

## 7. Limitation of Liability

### 7.1 General Limitations

| Liability Type | Limit |
|---------------|-------|
| Direct damages | Annual fees paid (12-month cap) |
| Indirect/consequential | Excluded (with exceptions) |
| Data breach (per incident) | [€X or insurance coverage] |

### 7.2 Exceptions to Limitations

Limitations do NOT apply to:
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct
- Breach of confidentiality
- Indemnification obligations
- IP infringement
- Personal injury

### 7.3 DORA-Specific Considerations

For DORA-regulated clients:
- Liability limitations must not impede regulatory compliance
- NCA access rights cannot be limited
- Audit rights cannot be financially constrained
- Exit rights must be preserved

## 8. Risk Allocation

### 8.1 Shared Responsibility Model

| Risk Area | Primary Responsible | Insurance/Indemnity |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Platform security | Provider | Provider's cyber insurance |
| Client's account security | Client | Client's insurance |
| Third-party breaches | Provider (for subcontractors) | Provider's insurance |
| Regulatory compliance | Shared | Both parties' insurance |
| Business interruption | Shared | Both parties' insurance |

### 8.2 Force Majeure

Neither party liable for failures due to:
- Natural disasters
- Acts of war or terrorism
- Government actions
- Pandemic (beyond reasonable control)
- Third-party infrastructure failures (Internet, utilities)

## 9. Claims Process

### 9.1 Incident to Claim Timeline

```
INCIDENT OCCURS
      │
      ▼
DETECTION & RESPONSE
(Immediate - per incident procedures)
      │
      ▼
DOCUMENTATION
(Within 24 hours)
      │
      ▼
INSURANCE NOTIFICATION
(Within policy-required timeframe)
      │
      ▼
CLIENT NOTIFICATION
(Per contract - 30 min to 24h)
      │
      ▼
CLAIM INVESTIGATION
(Insurance carrier)
      │
      ▼
RESOLUTION
(Per claim specifics)
```

### 9.2 Client Claim Submission

If Client believes they have a claim:
1. Notify Provider in writing within 30 days of discovering issue
2. Provide detailed description of alleged damages
3. Provider acknowledges within 5 business days
4. Investigation completed within 30 days
5. Resolution per contract terms
