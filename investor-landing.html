The Problem

Infrastructure Bottleneck in Systematic Trading

Professional systematic teams spend months building infrastructure before deploying their first strategy.

⏱

Time-to-Production

Teams spend 6-12 months building research, execution, and risk infrastructure before deploying their first live strategy.

🔒

Security Concerns

Most platforms require sharing broker API keys with cloud services, creating security and custody risks that deter institutional adoption.

📋

Compliance Burden

Regulated entities face increasing demand for governance evidence, audit trails, and vendor due diligence documentation.

💰

High Development Cost

Building institutional-grade quant infrastructure in-house requires €500K-€1M+ annual investment in specialized engineering talent.

📊

Sim-to-Live Gap

Fixed slippage models and unrealistic backtests lead to significant performance discrepancies between simulation and live trading.

⚠️

Tail Risk Blindness

Traditional ML optimizes average returns, ignoring catastrophic tail risks that can wipe out years of gains in a single drawdown event.

Our Solution

CCEA: Cloud-Controlled Execution Architecture

A novel architectural pattern that separates research/monitoring (Cloud) from execution (Agent), enabling secure, procurement-friendly deployment.

🏛️

Unique Architectural Moat

Cloud provides research, simulation, and monitoring. Agent runs in customer environment and performs any live execution via customer's own broker accounts. Cloud never stores credentials and never sends live trading instructions. This is designed to position CustodiaCloud as a Software/ICT Provider — not an execution service or investment advisor.

🔬

Research & Simulation

Comprehensive backtesting with 6-9 factor dynamic slippage models, L3 LOB simulation, and market impact modeling calibrated to real execution data.

🛡️

Risk-First Design

CVaR-aware reinforcement learning, local kill switch, pre-trade controls, and risk guards enforce limits before any order is placed.

📦

Signed Artifacts

Immutable, versioned strategy deployments with SBOM and provenance tracking. Agent verifies signatures before execution.

📋

Governance Evidence

Evidence exports designed to support client procurement and operational reviews. Change audit trails, retention policies, RBAC.

Technology

Novel Technical Innovations

Peer-reviewed research translated into production-ready implementation, with extensive automated test coverage.

Distributional PPO + CVaR

Risk-constrained RL optimizing CVaR-weighted returns, not just expected value. 21-51 quantile value distribution.

Twin Critics Architecture

Dual value networks with pessimistic aggregation, reducing overestimation bias in trading decisions.

UPGD Continual Learning

Utility-preserving gradient descent prevents catastrophic forgetting across market regime changes.

Conformal Prediction

Distribution-free uncertainty bounds with finite-sample coverage guarantees, even under distribution shift.

Multi-Factor TCA

6-9 factor parametric slippage models adapting to real-time market conditions per asset class.

L3 LOB Simulation

Full microstructure simulation: queue position, fill probability, market impact, dark pool routing.
